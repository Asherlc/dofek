How it’s calculated · HRmax proxy

Dofek applies Treff’s equation to bands based on profile maximum heart rate. These bands approximate ride intensity.

Easy
Below 80% HRmax
Middle
80% to below 90% HRmax
High
90% HRmax and above

The 2.00 line is Treff’s descriptive reference for training distribution.
